# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Remote teams and distributed coworkers who need a spatial collaboration tool. They open the app during work hours to join a shared 2D room — seeing where colleagues are, walking up to someone to start a conversation, and occupying a virtual office together. Context is focused professional work: laptop on a desk, ambient office or home-office lighting, multi-tab browser with this as a persistent tab.

## Product Purpose

A 2D metaverse for remote teams that turns flat video-call culture into a spatial, proximity-based experience. Users create rooms, place elements, and move avatars in real time via WebSocket. Success looks like: a team opens the app instead of Slack huddles because presence feels more natural here.

## Brand Personality

Minimal · Clean · Focused. The interface should feel like a sharp professional tool — confident in its restraint, never loud. Typography does the heavy lifting; color is used sparingly and with intent. The product earns trust through precision, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- **Gather.town** — the pixelated, game-like aesthetic reads as a toy. This product is a tool for real work; it should never feel casual or juvenile.
- Overly complex UIs with competing visual elements (Discord-style sidebar overload).
- Generic corporate blandness (Teams/Zoom sterility) — clean ≠ soulless.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — Every element earns its pixel. If a control doesn't immediately communicate its purpose, simplify it.
2. **Presence, not performance** — The spatial experience should feel natural and ambient, not gamified. No health bars, no XP, no pixel art.
3. **Quiet confidence** — The interface recedes so the collaboration shines. Restraint signals competence.
4. **Speed is a feature** — Perceived and real performance are both first-class. Transitions are swift (≤200ms), loads are instant-feeling, and nothing jitters.
5. **Progressive disclosure** — Show what's needed now, reveal depth on demand. A new user should feel comfortable in 30 seconds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text, 3:1 for large text). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keyboard navigation for all core flows (auth, space management, avatar movement). Color must never be the sole indicator of state.
